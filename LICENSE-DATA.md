# Data licences

**Code** in this repository is licensed under the European Union Public Licence v1.2 (`LICENSE`).

**Data** produced by the collectors keeps the licence of its source. Each dataset's `datapackage.json`
names the publisher, the licence, a link to the licence and the attribution to use.

| Dataset | Publisher | Licence | Attribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ctgov_ireland` | U.S. National Library of Medicine | Public data, [ClinicalTrials.gov terms](https://clinicaltrials.gov/about-site/terms-conditions) | Source: ClinicalTrials.gov, U.S. National Library of Medicine. |
| `ema_shortages` | European Medicines Agency | Re-use with acknowledgement, [EMA legal notice](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/about-us/legal-notice) | Source: European Medicines Agency (EMA). |
| `hpra_medicines` | Health Products Regulatory Authority | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Source: Health Products Regulatory Authority (HPRA), CC BY 4.0. |

Data compiled by Priya Life Science itself (for example, checked site locations) will be released under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), credited to "Airmid Éireann, Priya Life Science".

Sources marked `blocked` in `sources/registry.json` are never collected or published here, and the
registry says why.

Nothing here is medical advice.
