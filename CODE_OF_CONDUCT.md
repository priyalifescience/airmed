# Code of conduct

Airmid Éireann is a small, public-interest data project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests,
email or anywhere else connected to the project) is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, including when you disagree;
- assume good intent, and explain rather than blame;
- keep discussion about the data and the code, never about people's identities or private lives;
- never post anyone's personal details, including your own;
- accept that maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules.

Harassment, insults, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report a problem, email **info@priyalifescience.com** with the subject "Airmed conduct". Reports are read by the
maintainers at Priya Life Science and handled in confidence.
